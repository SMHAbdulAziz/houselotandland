Questions
When exactly do I get paid?

When the deal for your lead funds at the title company and House Lot & Land receives its funds, we pay you $500 within 10 business days (by Zelle, check or another method we agree on). Your page shows each step: submitted, verified, funded, paid.

What if the deal never closes?

Then there's no payout. Many sellers change their minds; that's why the payout is tied to funding. Your lead stays protected for 12 months after we verify it, so if it closes later in that window, you're still paid.

What should I say to the owner?

Just that you know a local company that buys houses as-is and ask if it's OK for them to call. Don't quote prices, make promises, sign anything or say you work for us. We take it from there.

Can I send a property that's listed with an agent?

Generally no. We're looking for off-market owners. Expired or withdrawn listings are fine if the owner is no longer under a listing agreement.

Do I need a license?

House Lot & Land does not require one to join. If you are a licensed agent, tell us at sign-up: your fee is paid to your sponsoring broker, as Texas rules require.

Program terms

Bird-Dog Program Terms

Version 2026-11-13.

  1. Payout. House Lot & Land Sales Co. LLC ("we") pays a flat $500 for each Qualified Lead that results in a Funded Deal. One payout per property, no matter how many deals or people are involved. The amount does not depend on the price or our profit.
  2. Qualified Lead. A lead qualifies only if all of these are true when you submit it: (a) the property is in Texas; (b) within the last 30 days you personally spoke with the owner, or an heir, executor or spouse with authority over the property; (c) that person agreed House Lot & Land may contact them; (d) you give a real reason the owner wants to sell; (e) you attach at least one current photo you took of the property; and (f) the property is not already in our system and was not submitted earlier by another bird-dog. We decide whether a lead qualifies after we verify it, and we may reject any lead, with the reason shown on your page.
  3. Funded Deal. A Funded Deal is a purchase, assignment, double close, subject-to or seller-financed acquisition of the property by us or our assignee that closes and funds through a title company within 12 months after we verify your lead. We pay within 10 business days after we receive our funds.
  4. What you may and may not do. You only introduce us to the owner. You may not make or discuss offers or prices, negotiate, sign or deliver documents, advertise or market any property, or say that you represent us or the owner. Never trespass, pressure anyone, or contact anyone who has asked not to be contacted. Information you send must be true and gathered lawfully.
  5. Independent referral source. You are not our employee, agent or partner, and you have no authority to bind us. You are responsible for your own taxes and expenses. We need a completed W-9 (through a secure link we send) before any payout, and we issue tax forms as the IRS requires.
  6. Licensed agents. If you hold a real estate license, you must tell us, and any fee is paid to your sponsoring broker, not to you directly.
  7. Your private link. Your bird-dog page link is your key. Keep it private. You're responsible for leads sent with it; call us if you think someone else has it and we'll replace it.
  8. Shabbat. We are closed for all business from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday. Leads can't be submitted during that time.
  9. Changes. We may change or end this program at any time for future leads. Leads we have already verified keep the terms in effect when you submitted them. You must be 18 or older to take part. Our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy also apply.
