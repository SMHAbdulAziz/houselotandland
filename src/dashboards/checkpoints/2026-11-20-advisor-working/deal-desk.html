Tax-sale properties (free)

Upcoming auctions: open taxsales.lgbs.com, filter to your county and "Scheduled for sale", switch to the list view, select the rows and copy. Didn't sell: open the county's struck-off or resale list (for example the Dallas County Property Division PDF) and copy its rows. Paste below; Claude turns each property into a lead. Up to about 40 properties per paste.

Before an auction, the owner can still sell and pay the taxes at closing: a motivated seller with a deadline. After a strike-off, the county and other taxing units own it; you buy through their resale. Texas gives the former owner a redemption period (2 years for homesteads and farmland, 180 days for most other property), so talk to an attorney before dealing with redemption rights.
