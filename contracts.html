Questions

Contract drafting FAQ

Is this a law firm?

No. This is self-help document software. You choose the document and answer the questions; the software fills in a Texas template. We can't give legal advice or tell you which document you need. For advice about your situation, talk to a Texas attorney.

Can I edit the document after I make it?

Your document opens as a printable page you can save as a PDF. If you need changes, make a new version with different answers.

How do subscriptions work?

Starter includes 3 documents each calendar month; Investor is unlimited. You get a private builder link after checkout. Cancel anytime by emailing or calling us; you keep access through the paid month.

Do the documents need a notary?

Documents that get recorded with the county, such as a memorandum of contract or an option, need a notary. The document shows where.

Can I use these outside Texas?

No. The templates are written for Texas property and Texas law only.
