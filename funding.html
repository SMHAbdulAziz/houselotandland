What lenders look for

  • Investment property. Business-purpose loans only. No borrower or family member can live in the property.
  • A real profit margin. Most flip lenders want at least $20,000 in projected profit after all costs.
  • Support for your ARV. Recent sold comps near the property, and a rehab budget that matches your scope.
  • Cash to close. Your down payment, closing costs and a reserve. The calculator above shows the first part.
  • Credit and experience. Requirements vary: some flip programs have no minimum score, and rental loans need no prior experience.
  • Not available for property in Utah, Idaho, Minnesota, Arizona, Massachusetts, New York, South Dakota or North Dakota.

Questions

Investor funding FAQ

Are you the lender?

No. House Lot & Land Sales Co. LLC is a private money broker. We review and package your deal and connect you with private lenders. The lender underwrites, approves and funds the loan and sets its final terms.

What does it cost?

Our broker fee is a percentage of the loan amount, paid at closing, and is agreed in writing before we submit your file. It's 1% on your first 3 funded loans. Lenders charge their own interest, points and fees, which we'll walk you through with their term sheet.

Does applying affect my credit?

Applying with us does not pull your credit. If you go forward with a lender, they will run a credit report as part of underwriting.

How fast can I close?

We review every application within one business day. After that, timing depends mostly on how quickly your documents come in and the lender's appraisal and title work. A complete file is the fastest path.

Do I need experience?

Not always. Long-term rental loans require no prior experience, and some fix-and-flip programs have no minimum credit score. Experience can improve your leverage and rate, so tell us about any deals you've done.

Can I use these loans for a home I'll live in?

No. These are business-purpose loans for investment property only. Neither you, a guarantor, nor any family member can live in the property.

How is the estimate on this page calculated?

The loan is the lower of 65% of after-repair value or 80% of the purchase price, a conservative starting point. Profit is ARV minus the purchase price, rehab, selling costs (8% for an agent sale), 5% finance costs and our broker fee. Some programs lend more; the lender decides after underwriting.

Which states do you serve?

We broker loans nationwide except for property in Utah, Idaho, Minnesota, Arizona, Massachusetts, New York, South Dakota and North Dakota.
