## How it deploys
- Cloudflare Worker `houselotandland` (account `leelane-aimhigh`), built by Workers Builds on every push to `main`.
- `wrangler.jsonc`: static files from the repo root; `src/worker.js` runs first only for `/api/*`, `/offer/*`, `/find`, `/sign/*`, `/doc/*`, `/order/*`.
- Custom domains `houselotandland.org` and `www.houselotandland.org`. DNS is on Cloudflare.
  Do NOT attach `msg` or `esign` to this Worker: they are Cloudflare tunnels to Lee's Mac (texting bridge).
- `.assetsignore` keeps `src/`, `wrangler.jsonc`, `*.md`, `form-handler.php` (unused, legacy) off the public site.
- Test locally: `npx wrangler dev --local` (D1 runs locally with `--persist-to`).

## Back office (houselotandland.org/office)

  (vars `ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN`, `ACCESS_AUD`, optional `OFFICE_EMAILS` in `wrangler.jsonc`). Empty vars = locked. Local testing: `OFFICE_DEV=1` in `.dev.vars`.

  workers and crews (status, W-9, agreement, insurance, jobs, pay owed/paid), links to every dashboard.
  Overview also shows Services & sales (revenue this month/30 days/year from `service_payments`, subscriptions and MRR, invoices paid, open jobs, unpaid, crew pay owed, upcoming jobs, recent payments).
  Property drawer "Email the owner or family": drafts from `office/owner-email.js` (owner-has-passed-away templates per track: wholesale cash, Sub-To take over payments, creative seller-finance; EN/ES), fills recipient/late owner from the property's contacts (roles heir/executor/spouse vs owner), opens a Gmail compose for houselotandland@gmail.com and logs the activity; hidden when every contact opted out. Footer uses `settings.mail_address` (CAN-SPAM) plus a reply-"remove" opt-out. The same card texts the short version (bridge templates `heir_wholesale`, `heir_subto`, `heir_creative`, EN/ES) only to contacts with `consent = 1`, never opted-out or litigator ones. Each contact row can record text consent by hand (PATCH `contact/<id>` `{consent, consent_source, consent_note}` stores `consent_json` {source, at, by, note} and logs it); refused on opted-out contacts.
  Jobs tab: every service order with filters; drawer schedules (no Saturdays, sets status Scheduled), assigns crew (`worker_jobs`), records payments, office notes.
  Quotes tab (test bookings show too, marked TEST, and never charge): Curb Appeal lots over 2 acres (and lot-size corrections on unpaid bookings). `curbQuoteSuggest` + `CURB_QUOTE` in `js/services-catalog.js` suggest lines; Harvey edits and saves (`POST /api/office/quote/<id>`, status `quoted`, `extra.quoteDoc`). The customer's order page shows the quote with Approve and pay (`POST /api/order/<token>/approve` makes a Stripe Checkout session; Shabbat-blocked). Harvey sends the link from Gmail (compose link prefilled); nothing is emailed automatically.

## Rules
- **Shabbat, without exception:** closed for all new business from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday (Dallas sunset, `js/shabbat.js`, shared by browser and Worker).
  The Worker refuses new-business POSTs (`NEW_BUSINESS` in `src/worker.js`: lead, loan, service orders, offer choices, signatures, new subscription documents) with 503 and the reopening time;
  `js/shabbat-banner.js` (on every page) shows the notice and countdown and hides forms. Paid customers keep access to order pages, documents, offer pages and Find my offer.
  No Curb Appeal work dates on Saturdays. The hourly scheduled task stops during Shabbat (STEP 0). Terms of Use: `terms.html` (section 2, English and Spanish). Privacy Policy: `privacy.html` (EN/ES, Nov 11, 2026; lists every data source and processor: Cloudflare, Stripe, Google, phone carrier/texting gateway, ElevenLabs call recordings, Anthropic, TaxBandits, data providers; SMS consent never shared). Update it when a new provider or data type is added. Both are linked in every page footer. Any new form or endpoint must follow this.
  `GET /api/hours` gives open/closed plus the spoken closed notice; the AI phone line's tool `check_business_hours` calls it on every call. The power-washing bid scan skips Saturdays.
