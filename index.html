Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about selling your property

What types of properties do you buy?

We buy houses, lots, land, and distressed properties in any condition - whether they're vacant or occupied. No property is too damaged or too small for us to consider.

How quickly can I get a cash offer?

We provide no-obligation cash offers within 24 hours of receiving your property information. The process is fast and straightforward.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No! We buy properties as-is. You don't need to make any repairs, clean up, or spend money fixing anything. We'll take care of everything.

How fast can we close?

We can close in as little as 7 days, depending on your timeline and preferences. We work around your schedule.

Is the cash offer really no-obligation?

Yes! Our cash offers are completely no-obligation. You can review the offer, ask questions, and decide if it's right for you with no pressure.

What if the property was inherited or is in probate?

We buy inherited properties often. Tell us who the owners or heirs are, and we'll explain the steps and work with the title company to get everyone to closing.

Will you buy the property yourselves?

We buy through a purchase contract. We may close on the property ourselves or assign our contract to another buyer before closing, and we always tell you this in writing. Either way, your price and closing terms stay the same.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater Dallas-Fort Worth area and surrounding communities. Contact us to see if we cover your specific location.
