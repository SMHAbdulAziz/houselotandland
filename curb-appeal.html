Questions

Curb appeal FAQ

Who does the work?

Local crews we vet and schedule ourselves. We coordinate everything, check the work and stand behind it.

What if it rains?

We'll move you to the next open day and let you know right away. Pressure washing and painting need dry weather.

Can I customize a package?

Yes. Add notes when you book, or call us. We'll quote anything extra before we do it.

Do I need to be home?

No. For front-yard work we don't need access. For backyards, tell us about gates, codes and pets.

What days do you work?

Sunday through Friday. We close for Shabbat from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday, so there is no work, booking or payment during that time. Friday jobs finish before sunset.

What areas do you serve?

Most of North Texas: Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, Denton, Rockwall, Kaufman, Ellis and Johnson counties. Ask about others.

Note. Package contents and prices are for typical properties; we'll confirm anything unusual before work starts. We don't perform work that requires a Texas license, including irrigation, electrical, pesticide application, tree work near power lines and roofing.
