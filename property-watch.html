Questions

Property watch FAQ

What's in an inspection report?

Exterior and interior photos of every elevation and room, utilities and meter status, occupancy evidence, damage, safety and code issues, lock and lockbox status, lawn condition and recommended actions with estimates.

How fast do we hear about problems?

Routine reports within 24 hours of the visit. Break-ins, active leaks, storm damage and code postings are reported the same day.

Can you follow our investor or agency guidelines?

Yes. Send us your guidelines and photo requirements and we'll follow them, including your naming conventions and upload process.

How is pricing set?

A per-inspection rate with volume discounts, plus an onboarding visit for each new property. Preservation work such as grass cuts, winterization, rekeys and debris removal is billed per job at the rates in your service agreement.

Which areas do you cover?

North Texas, centered on Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton counties, with coverage of surrounding counties at a small trip charge.
